How it works

A lightweight, transparent experiment with no prediction claims.

  1. Decode a video locally using the browser.
  2. Sample frames on a small grayscale canvas.
  3. Calculate the mean absolute change between adjacent frames.
  4. Plot motion intensity over time and export the readings.

Privacy

Video files stay on this device and are not sent to the app backend. Clearing or closing the page discards measurements.

Limitations

  • Camera motion and lighting changes affect results.
  • Movement score is not gait speed, cadence, or symptom severity.
  • There is no validated classifier and no patient-level diagnosis.
  • Videos unsupported by your browser cannot be processed.

For a clinical concern, contact a qualified health professional.
